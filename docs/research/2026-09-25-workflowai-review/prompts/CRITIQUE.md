# Critique of the two positions and the subject

Frame `task:wai-critique`. Read `COMMON.md` first.

Inputs: the subject, `positions/A.md` and `positions/B.md`.

Work:
1. For each finding of each position: CONFIRMED, WRONG (with evidence) or OVERSTATED. Check the
   triage class it was given.
2. What did both positions miss? Look hardest at section 1.5 (selection), section 2 (script
   supervision) and the tech-debt boundary of section 6.
3. End with one line: `Critic verdict: PASS | RECOMMENDATION | FAIL - <reason>`. A FAIL needs a
   reproduction or a cited contradiction per claim (AGENTS.md section 2).

Output: `critique.md`.
