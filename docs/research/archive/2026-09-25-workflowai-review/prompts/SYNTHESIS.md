Frame `task:wai-synthesis`. Read `COMMON.md` first. You are the senior synthesiser of this cycle.

Inputs: the subject, both positions and `critique.md`.

Work:
1. One disposition per distinct finding: ACCEPT, REJECT (with the reason) or DEFER, with its
   triage class. Keep dissent visible.
2. A revision proposal for `workflowAI.md`: exact replacement text per changed passage. Do not edit

   `docs/ops/PROBLEMS.md` if anything blocks.
4. A final line: `Synthesis verdict: READY TO APPLY | APPLY WITH OWNER QUESTIONS | NOT READY`.

Output: `synthesis.md` (up to 200 lines).
