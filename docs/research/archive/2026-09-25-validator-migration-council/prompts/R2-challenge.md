# Round 2: challenge of one round-1 report

- Frame `task:vmc-r2-<a|b|c>`, parent-scope `program:validator-migration-council`. Your one role: challenger of zone X (owner §23). Read
  `prompts/COMMON.md` first; it binds you.
- The owner's launch message names `Target: A`, `B` or `C`. You did not write that zone's round-1
  files, and your model is of another family than their author's (owner §23: "No one challenges
  their own report").

## Inputs

All of `round1/`, which is frozen: the target zone's files in full, and the other zones' files
for context.

## Work

For every major proposal of the target zone, answer the fifteen questions of §23, each with
evidence (COMMON section 4). The fixed form per proposal:

`<proposal, cited by path:line>`: AGREE, PARTLY AGREE, DISAGREE or UNKNOWN; then the answers to
questions 1-15, each one line or marked "n/a" with the reason.

Where the target contradicts another zone, say so with both citations. Do not resolve it; the
issue matrix and round 3 do that.

## Output

`round2/challenge-<X>.md` (at most 250 lines).

## Do not

- Open another `round2/challenge-*` before yours is finished (COMMON section 6).
- Rewrite the target's proposal: challenge it.
