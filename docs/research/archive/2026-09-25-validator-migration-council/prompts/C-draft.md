# Closing step b: the draft decision (PROTO-DEC-0053 step b)

- Frame `task:vmc-draft`, parent-scope `program:validator-migration-council`. Your one role: drafter (owner §25). Read `prompts/COMMON.md` first; it
  binds you.
- The drafter is the round-3 synthesiser the owner names (README model table): the Anthropic flagship synthesiser, at
  the highest appropriate effort (owner §25).

## Inputs

The three `round3/synthesis-*.md` files, and the frozen corpus of `round3/CORPUS.txt`. Each
divergence is resolved by evidence, so read the corpus, not only the syntheses.

## Work

Write one entry per decision point, with the ten fields of §25: ISSUE, SYNTHESIS A, SYNTHESIS B,
SYNTHESIS C, AGREEMENT, DIVERGENCE, EVIDENCE, PROPOSED RESOLUTION, WHY, WHAT WOULD FALSIFY IT.
- Cover every question of §24 as at least one decision point.
- Minority objections stay visible (owner §25).
- Number the points (D-01, D-02, ...): the critics answer point by point.

## Output

`draft-decision.md`. A draft is not a decision (owner §25; PROTO-DEC-0053 item 2).
