# Worklog Archive

Cold storage for worklog entries moved out of `.ai/worklog/`. Never trimmed,
never summarized away.

Entries keep the exact text they had when they were current. Add the agent name
and the date they were archived; change nothing else.

Information needed to understand an accepted decision belongs in
`.ai/DECISIONS.md`, not here. This file is history, not authority.

## Archiving procedure

1. A journal passed its 150-line limit, or `.ai/worklog/` passed 30 files.
   `validate-protocol.ps1` reports both.
2. Cut the oldest entries from that journal.
3. Paste them below, under a heading naming the agent and the archive date.
4. Leave the newest entries in place, enough to explain the current state.
5. Never rewrite an entry while moving it.
